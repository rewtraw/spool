On the television, at home and away.

Players that speak to Jellyfin, Infuse among them, see the library as Spool knows it. A film comes from the machine that holds it, and a small version is there for a slow line.

On the television

Plays what it files.

Spool can answer as a Jellyfin server, so a player such as Infuse on Apple TV opens the library directly. There is no second server to run and no folders to share. Playing on a television

Found by itself

Players on the same network see Spool without being given an address. It has its own port and its own sign-in, which opens nothing but playback.

The library as Spool knows it

Posters, cast, scores, collections, seasons and episodes, with search, recently added, continue watching and next up.

Every file as it is

Nothing is transcoded. Each version of a film is offered, with its subtitles, and where you stopped is remembered for next time.

Where a film comes from

From the machine that holds it.

A player knows one server, Spool. When a film is on another machine, such as a NAS, Spool hands the player a ticket for that one film and the player takes it from there. Nothing passes through a machine it did not need to. Playing from the helper

At home

A player on the same network as the NAS takes the film from the NAS. Seeking is as quick as the disk, and where you stopped is still kept by Spool.

Away

Over Tailscale a phone is sent to the NAS's Tailscale address, and at home to its home one. Spool tells which by where the phone really is, not by how it connected.

One switch

There are no addresses to list. One switch says whether players are sent to a machine, and a player that cannot reach it gets the film through Spool, as before.

Compact versions

The whole film at home, a small one for the road.

Spool can make a smaller copy of a film or an episode and keep it beside the original, as a version a player can choose. The original is never touched. Compact versions

Made on the video hardware

On an Apple silicon Mac a 47 GB film on disc becomes 5 GB at 1080p in about ten minutes, with the processor left nearly idle.

Kept as a version

HDR, every sound track, subtitles and chapters are carried over. Players are offered both; searches and upgrades go on looking at the original.

One, some or all

Make one from a film's page, several from the library, everything that would gain from one, or each download as it arrives. Progress is in Activity.
