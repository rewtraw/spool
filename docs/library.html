Your library, as you keep it.

Spool takes the folders you have, wherever they are, and changes nothing it was not asked to. Then it keeps them in order.

What you already have

Takes your folders as it finds them.

In more than one place

Add disks and shares beside the main library, managed or only read.

Away means left alone

A NAS that is off shows as offline. Nothing is fetched again.

Moved when you say

Send titles to another location: copied whole, checked, then cleared.

One title, two places

A film on two disks is one entry, offered to players as versions.

A profile for each place

An archive of discs and an everyday disk want different things.

Leaves the rest of a folder

Trailers, extras and your own posters stay. An upgrade replaces the film only.
