It downloads on purpose.

Every release gets a verdict and a reason. What Spool takes is checked first, unpacked as it arrives, and remembered after it has gone.

Choosing

Knows why it took one and not another.

A reason for every release

Accepted, or rejected and why. "Why not that one" always has an answer.

Whole seasons, one source

A pack or one group's full run comes before a scatter of episodes.

Sizes you choose

Profiles carry a size target. Oversized releases are the last resort.

Original language first

Dubs rank last, and anime keeps its own episode numbering.

Reads awkward names

No year, no id, or garbled non-Latin text: still recognised.

More than one version

Keep a 4K copy beside a small one. Upgrades leave the others alone.

Fetching

Careful with your time and your disk.

Looks before it downloads

A release is sampled on your servers first. Expired posts are skipped in seconds.

Unpacks as it arrives

Uncompressed posts are unpacked volume by volume, in about half the room.

Cautious by default

It starts in a mode that changes nothing, and will not write when the disk is missing.

The archive

Delete to make room. Get it back later.

Kept after it has gone

Spool keeps the small file that describes each release it downloads.

No indexer needed

Saved releases join every search, even when an indexer is down.

Honest about what is left

It checks a saved release is still on Usenet before offering it.
